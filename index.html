In short: The app lets you personalize Android home-screen widgets. Some functions use information you choose to provide or grant access to. Advertising services may process device and usage data independently of your personal widget content.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Bhagwati Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the True Glass Widgets Android application (the "App") and this privacy policy page.

The App provides home-screen widgets including clocks, calendars, launchers, notes, utility controls, device information, personalization, and optional internet-based content. Some features are only available on supported devices or after you grant the relevant permissions.

The information accessed depends on the features you choose to use, your Android version, and the permissions you grant. A reference to an optional capability below does not mean that every version of the App requests it.

2. Information the App may process

Widget settings and content

When you create or customize widgets, the App may process preferences such as selected widget designs, sizes, colors, app shortcuts, widget positions, saved notes, checklists, habits, reminders, QR-code content, and similar information you enter. This data is used to display and operate your selected widgets.

Photos, wallpapers, and selected files

If you use photo widgets, photo frames, wallpaper matching, glass alignment, or document shortcuts, the App may access images or files you select. Selected content is used to create a preview, display a widget, align wallpaper-based effects, or open a chosen item. Files you share or select may also be processed by the Android system or apps you choose to open them with.

Device and system information

Device-monitoring or quick-control widgets may read information available through Android APIs, such as battery status, storage availability, memory estimates, device model, Android version, connectivity status, sensor readings, and supported audio or display settings.

Personal information you choose to use

Optional features may access selected contacts, calendar entries, activity or step data, app usage statistics, and location information to provide the corresponding widgets. For example, a weather widget may need an approximate or precise location, while a contact shortcut may need a selected contact. These features require the relevant Android access or another explicit selection where applicable.

Internet requests

Online features—such as weather, finance, currency exchange, news, sports, or other live-information widgets—may send requests to external data providers. Depending on the provider and feature, those requests may include a location or city, a selected ticker or currency pair, a search term, technical network information, or other details needed to deliver the requested content.

Advertising and technical information

If the App displays advertisements, the advertising SDK may collect or receive technical data such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad views, and diagnostic information. See Section 4 for details.

Optional account, purchases, and synchronization features

If an installed version of the App offers account sign-in, online backup, premium purchases, or subscription services, the data necessary for the feature may be handled by the relevant account, billing, or synchronization provider. Payment processing through Google Play is subject to Google's terms and privacy practices. We do not need your complete payment card information to activate a purchase made through Google Play.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App may display advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the following categories of information to provide ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • App interactions, including ad views, taps, and app usage events;
  • Diagnostic information about performance and technical problems; and
  • Device or account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID, when available and permitted.

Depending on your region, consent choices, settings, and applicable law, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Google's advertising services may use cookies and similar technologies in relevant web or ad contexts and may process device identifiers, IP addresses, and other technical information.

Where required, we will request advertising consent through the App's consent interface. You can also review advertising-related controls in Android settings. Resetting or deleting an advertising identifier may affect ad personalization but does not necessarily prevent all advertising or technical processing.

For more information, see Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps and sites using its services, and Google Advertising Privacy.

5. Internet services and external links

Some widgets show online information supplied by third parties. The App may communicate with the providers needed to retrieve requested data. Those providers may receive technical request information, including IP address, and any feature-specific information needed to provide a response.

When you tap a shortcut to a website, social platform, streaming service, map application, or another app, that service operates under its own privacy policy. We do not control the privacy practices of independent third-party apps or websites.

If external providers, cloud synchronization, analytics services, or additional advertising services are added or changed, this policy should be updated to identify material new data uses or sharing.

6. How information may be shared

Information may be processed by or disclosed to the following categories of recipients when needed:

  • Advertising providers: Google AdMob and its authorized advertising technology partners for ad serving, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Requested content providers: External services supplying weather, market prices, news, transport, or other online widget information.
  • Platforms and apps you choose: Android, Google Play, file providers, browsers, and applications you open or link through a widget.
  • Legal and safety recipients: Where disclosure is required by applicable law or is necessary to protect users, rights, and service security.

Information that you intentionally share—such as a QR code containing contact details, Wi-Fi credentials, or a payment link—can be read by people or applications with whom you choose to share it. Review QR-code contents before sharing.

7. Data storage, retention, and deletion

Widget configurations and user-created content may be stored in the App's local data storage so widgets continue working between sessions. Information remains for as long as it is needed for the corresponding feature, until you delete it through an available in-app control, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

If you enable a separately offered cloud synchronization or backup feature, copies may remain with its provider until deleted under that feature's controls and applicable retention policies. We will describe any such storage and deletion procedure in the App if the feature is offered.

Information processed by Google, external content providers, or other independent services is subject to their own retention policies. Removing the App does not automatically erase records independently retained by those providers.

To request assistance regarding personal information controlled by Bhagwati Studio, contact us using the details below. We will review requests and respond as required by applicable law.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Optional permissions: Allow or revoke permissions and special access in Android settings.
  • Widget content: Change or delete notes, shortcuts, and preferences using controls available in the App.
  • Advertising preferences: Use the consent choices offered in the App where applicable and controls available from Android and Google.
  • Location-based widgets: Disable location access or stop using widgets requiring it.
  • Data removal: Remove saved content, clear the App's storage, uninstall the App, or contact us for requests concerning data we control.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restrictions on the processing of personal information. Contact us to exercise rights that apply to your circumstances.

9. Security

We aim to limit data access to what is needed for a feature and to use the protection mechanisms made available by Android and relevant service providers. Network communications with services that support HTTPS should use encrypted transport. No method of storage or internet communication can be guaranteed completely secure.

Take care when saving sensitive information in home-screen widgets, which may be visible to anyone who can see your unlocked device. Avoid placing passwords, OTPs, or other secret information in widgets or sharable QR codes.

10. Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose customization and utility application and is not designed specifically for children. Any advertising served to users must follow applicable age-related laws and Google Play requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us.

11. International data processing

Third-party services used by the App, including Google advertising products and online data providers, may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Applicable privacy and data protection rules may vary by location. Those providers describe their safeguards in their own privacy documentation.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this policy when the App's features, data practices, providers, or legal requirements change. The updated effective date will appear near the top of this page. For material changes, we will provide additional notice in the App when required.
